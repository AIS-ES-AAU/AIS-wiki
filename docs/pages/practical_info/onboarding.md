# Onboarding for new staff
Welcome to AI and Sound at AAU! On this page you can find help you with some practical matters as well as some general tips for your start at AAU.

## Section meetings
The head of section will host section meetings at a regular interval. 
At these meetings we go through various decisions and news related to our section.

## Staff card
To access the buildings outside of normal opening hours you will need a staff card.
Information about how to get this card can be found [here](https://www.en.campusservice.aau.dk/practical-information/aaucard).

If you want access to the labs you will have to ask the technical assistants in the lab to grant you access and provide your staff card number.
